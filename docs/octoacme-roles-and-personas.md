# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Engineering Managers

### Role Summary
Engineering Managers support engineering teams and oversee delivery health, team capacity, and technical execution.

### Responsibilities
- Plan team capacity and address staffing or skills gaps
- Monitor delivery health and help resolve execution blockers
- Coach Developers and promote engineering standards and quality practices
- Escalate technical or resourcing risks and propose mitigation options

### Goals
- Enable sustainable, predictable engineering delivery
- Support a healthy, effective development team
- Balance technical quality, capacity, and delivery commitments

### Typical Communication
- Regular one-on-ones and engineering team discussions
- Capacity and delivery-health reviews
- Escalations and trade-off discussions with project and product leads

### Interaction with Other Roles
- Partners with Project Managers on schedule, capacity, and risk trade-offs
- Works with Product Managers to assess scope feasibility and sequencing
- Supports Developers with blockers, coaching, and engineering standards
- Coordinates with QA/Testing on quality risks and sustainable validation plans
- Shares material delivery or capacity risks with relevant stakeholders

---

## UX/UI Designers

### Role Summary
UX/UI Designers shape usable, accessible experiences by translating user needs and product goals into flows, interactions, and visual designs.

### Responsibilities
- Create user flows, wireframes, interaction designs, and design assets
- Validate usability and incorporate research or feedback
- Consider accessibility and consistency across user experiences
- Clarify design behavior and edge cases for implementation

### Goals
- Make product experiences intuitive, useful, and accessible
- Ensure designs address user needs and product outcomes
- Reduce usability gaps between design intent and delivered behavior

### Typical Communication
- Design reviews, prototypes, and annotated specifications
- User research findings and usability test results
- Design handoffs and implementation discussions

### Interaction with Other Roles
- Partners with Product Managers to refine user problems, priorities, and acceptance criteria
- Works with Developers to assess feasibility and clarify design details during implementation
- Coordinates with Project Managers on design dependencies, reviews, and delivery timing
- Collaborates with QA/Testing on usability, accessibility, and design-focused validation
- Shares research insights and design decisions with relevant stakeholders

---

## Customer Support / Success Representatives

### Role Summary
Customer Support / Success Representatives connect project teams with customer needs, adoption concerns, and post-release feedback.

### Responsibilities
- Share recurring customer questions, pain points, and product feedback
- Prepare support documentation and customer-facing guidance
- Identify support and training needs ahead of releases
- Track post-release feedback and communicate emerging issues

### Goals
- Help customers adopt and gain value from delivered changes
- Enable clear, consistent support for new and existing capabilities
- Close the feedback loop between customers and product teams

### Typical Communication
- Customer feedback summaries and support trend reports
- Release notes, help content, and readiness briefings
- Issue escalations and post-release updates

### Interaction with Other Roles
- Informs Product Managers' prioritization with customer needs and field insights
- Coordinates with Project Managers on readiness activities and stakeholder updates
- Shares known issues and troubleshooting context with Developers
- Works with QA/Testing to clarify customer-impacting scenarios and validate support guidance
- Prepares customer communications and gathers feedback with relevant stakeholders

---

## Security / Compliance Leads

### Role Summary
Security / Compliance Leads advise teams on security risks, regulatory obligations, and controls needed to deliver responsibly.

### Responsibilities
- Identify security, privacy, and compliance requirements and risks
- Advise on threat mitigation and secure design practices
- Define or coordinate security and compliance review checkpoints
- Support incident readiness and remediation planning

### Goals
- Protect customer and organizational data
- Meet applicable security and compliance obligations
- Address risks early without creating avoidable delivery surprises

### Typical Communication
- Risk assessments and security review findings
- Compliance requirements and control checklists
- Remediation plans and incident-readiness guidance

### Interaction with Other Roles
- Helps Project Managers track security and compliance risks, dependencies, and review timing
- Aligns with Product Managers on regulatory constraints and customer commitments
- Partners with Developers on secure implementation and remediation
- Coordinates with QA/Testing on security-focused validation and evidence
- Advises relevant stakeholders on material risks, controls, and readiness decisions

---

## Data Analysts / BI Partners

### Role Summary
Data Analysts / BI Partners help teams define meaningful measures and use reliable data to understand product and project outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define measurement plans and success metrics with product teams
- Advise on data availability, quality, and interpretation
- Coordinate analytics requirements and telemetry needs
- Report outcomes and evaluate experiments or changes

### Goals
- Make product and delivery decisions using trustworthy evidence
- Measure whether changes achieve their intended outcomes
- Make performance and outcome trends understandable to teams

### Typical Communication
- KPI definitions, dashboards, and analysis summaries
- Measurement plans and experiment readouts
- Data-quality findings and instrumentation requirements

### Interaction with Other Roles
- Works with Product Managers to define KPIs and evaluate product outcomes
- Partners with Project Managers to improve status reporting and communicate measurable progress
- Coordinates with Developers on telemetry and event instrumentation
- Aligns with QA/Testing on data validation and expected measurement behavior
- Presents relevant findings and limitations to stakeholders

---

## Release / Operations Coordinators

### Role Summary
Release / Operations Coordinators, including SRE representatives, help teams prepare, deploy, and operate changes reliably.

### Responsibilities
- Coordinate release schedules, readiness checks, and deployment dependencies
- Confirm monitoring, support, and rollback plans are in place
- Communicate operational requirements and deployment procedures
- Support operational monitoring and post-release follow-up

### Goals
- Make releases predictable and recoverable
- Protect service reliability during and after deployment
- Ensure teams are prepared to detect and respond to operational issues

### Typical Communication
- Release plans, readiness checklists, and deployment updates
- Monitoring expectations and operational runbooks
- Incident, rollback, and post-release communications

### Interaction with Other Roles
- Coordinates with Project Managers on release timing, dependencies, and readiness status
- Works with Developers on deployment execution, monitoring, and rollback procedures
- Aligns with Product Managers on release scope and customer-facing timing
- Partners with QA/Testing to confirm validation and release criteria are complete
- Coordinates incident and release communications with Support and relevant stakeholders
