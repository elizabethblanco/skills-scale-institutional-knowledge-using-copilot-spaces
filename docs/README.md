# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

## Overview

This README centralizes OctoAcme project management knowledge and links to the
process documents used throughout the project lifecycle. Use it to find a
concise summary of how projects are initiated, planned, delivered, and improved,
then consult the linked guides for details.

## Project Management Process Summary

OctoAcme follows a lightweight, iterative lifecycle: initiation confirms the
business need, stakeholders, measurable success criteria, and a go/no-go
decision; planning turns approved work into a prioritized, estimated backlog
with acceptance criteria, milestones, dependencies, and a Definition of Done.
Projects are coordinated by a Project Manager (PM) and Product Manager (PdM),
with Developers, QA/testing, and stakeholders contributing according to their
roles.

Execution uses a project board, small pull requests, and regular team rhythms:
daily standups, weekly delivery and PM/PdM syncs, and sprint or milestone
demos. Progress is tracked against milestones and success metrics, with tests,
CI checks, review, and QA built into delivery. Risks and dependencies are
recorded and reviewed weekly; status updates keep stakeholders informed, while
blockers escalate from team triage through the PM and Product Lead to the
sponsor when business impact warrants it.

Releases require acceptance criteria and CI/security checks to pass, release
notes and rollback plans to be ready, and smoke tests and post-deployment
verification. Teams deploy to staging before production where applicable and
notify stakeholders and support. Retrospectives after sprints, releases,
milestones, and incidents capture learnings as owned action items, which are
tracked and revisited to drive continuous improvement.
